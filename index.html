🔍 What Is This Tool?

Windows Firewall Rule Exporter Tool is a free, easy-to-use program for Windows that helps you manage your firewall rules. Think of it as a backup assistant for your computer's security settings. It lets you save a copy of all your firewall rules, so if you ever need to reinstall Windows, get a new computer, or accidentally mess something up, you can quickly restore everything to how it was before.

You don't need to be a computer expert to use this tool. It's designed for everyone — from home users who want to protect their family computer to IT professionals who manage many computers at work.

⚡ Key Features

💾 Backup Firewall Rules

Save all your Windows Firewall rules to a single file. Perfect for making a safety copy before making changes.

🔄 Restore Firewall Rules

Put your rules back exactly how they were. Great for when you get a new computer or after a system reset.

📄 Export to CSV

Convert your firewall rules to a spreadsheet-friendly format. You can open it in Excel or Google Sheets to review, sort, or print.

📋 Export to XML

Save your rules in a detailed, structured format that's easy to share or use in other programs.

❓ Why Would You Need This?

Here are some everyday situations where this tool really helps:

  • 🖥️
    Getting a new computer: Instead of manually recreating all your firewall rules, just back them up from your old PC, then restore them on your new one.
  • 🔧
    Testing changes: Before you try a new program or change settings, make a backup. If something goes wrong, you can quickly go back to how things were.
  • 📋
    Keeping records: Export your rules to CSV to keep a simple list of what's allowed and blocked on your computer.
  • 🔄
    System recovery: If your computer crashes and you need to reinstall Windows, you can restore all your firewall settings afterward.

🔒 Safety First

Your firewall is an important part of your computer's security. This tool only reads your firewall settings — it doesn't change anything unless you explicitly tell it to. When you restore or export rules, it's just copying information. The tool is designed to be safe and won't harm your system.

If you're ever unsure about something, make a backup first! That way, you can always go back to how things were.
